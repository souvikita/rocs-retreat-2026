Science speed dating, in short

Short one-to-one conversations about your science, with a different colleague every round, until you have met everyone. Your one-page poster is the conversation starter: bring it printed and keep it with you.

The numbers

Slots
Five slots: three of 45 minutes, then two of one hour
Rounds
25 in total: 5 in every slot
One round
7 minutes talking in the 45-minute slots, 9½ in the one-hour slots; then a signal, then 90 seconds to move
Who you meet
All 25 of your colleagues, each exactly once
Tables
13 tables in a line, numbered 1 to 13, one chair on side A and one on side B

What to do

  1. Before the retreat, upload your one-page poster as a PDF via the Posters tab, and print a copy to bring along.
  2. Open this page on your phone, choose your name under My schedule. It shows who you meet in each round and at which table and side.
  3. Before the first round, go to your table for round 1.
  4. Talk until the signal. A good split: 3 minutes each on what you work on and an idea you would like to pursue, the rest on where you could help each other. Anything that deserves longer continues over coffee or at dinner.
  5. At the signal, stand up and move to your next table. The page tells you where; the rule below gives the same answer.

The moving rule

stays at table 1, side A, all day. Everyone else moves one seat along the line after every round:

  1. Side A: move up one table (1 → 2 → … → 13).
  2. At table 13 on side A: cross over to table 13, side B.
  3. Side B: move down one table (13 → 12 → … → 1).
  4. At table 1 on side B: cross over to table 2, side A.

This is why nobody meets the same person twice. If someone is away for a round, their partner simply has a coffee break; nothing else changes.
